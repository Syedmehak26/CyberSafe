03 / TAKE THE NEXT STEP

A clear plan when something goes wrong

Start with safety, preserve relevant evidence, then choose the right reporting route.

Your first steps

If someone is threatening or blackmailing you

You deserve help. The abuse is not your fault. You do not have to handle it alone.

  • Save relevant messages, profile details, URLs, dates and screenshots where safe.
  • Do not pay, send more material, or share OTPs and passwords.
  • Report the account and content through the platform’s official reporting tools.
  • Report the incident through the official cybercrime portal or contact police as appropriate.
  • If there is immediate danger, seek emergency assistance. If a minor is involved, involve a trusted adult and appropriate authorities.
